Rating guide

Attainment bands

Mainstream and G&T: below 50% Below; 50–69% In line; 70%+ Above Expectation.

SoD / PoD: below 31% Below; 31–70% In line; 71%+ Above Expectation.

Each student is banded with their own group's thresholds. "At least in line" includes Above. In line only and Below are exclusive.

Attainment judgment

  • Outstanding: 75%+ Above
  • Very Good: 75%+ In line and 61–74% Above
  • Good: 75%+ In line and 50–60% Above
  • Acceptable: 75%+ In line, under 50% Above
  • Weak: 16–74% In line
  • Very Weak: 0–15% In line

"In line" here means at least in line (includes Above).

Progress rating

Compares the same subject across the two exams using ten-point bands: 0–10, 11–20 … 91–100.

Better: end band higher, or both scores 91–100 (90.x does not qualify). Expected: same band. Weak: end band lower.

  • Outstanding: 75%+ Better
  • Very Good: 75%+ Expected-or-Better and 61–74% Better
  • Good: 75%+ Expected-or-Better and 50–60% Better
  • Acceptable: 75%+ Expected-or-Better, under 50% Better
  • Weak: 16–74% Expected-or-Better
  • Very Weak: 0–15% Expected-or-Better

Achiever groups use the 2025-26 score in the same subject: High achiever above 85%, Low achiever below 40%, Middle otherwise.
